What We Do

We turn new
into known.

Universities already have an abundance of offices and services for incoming students to assimilate to campus life. However, too few students understand how to navigate those systems, and perhaps more importantly, those systems do not in and of themselves create communities. Student Horizons connects students to the resources they need, while building the communities they want. Student Horizons is currently actively recruiting Student Ambassadors for Northeastern University, and is seeking expansion opportunities to alternative universities as well.

The Role

What a Student Ambassador does.

Meet Students Where They're At

Answering their questions in the greatest capacity possible.
What they need to buy, where they need to go, and who they need to meet, in order to maximize their success on campus.

Reach Out First

Most people who need help hesitate to send the first message.
Be proactive, reach out, and build a bond that'll last across semesters.

How to Join

Five Steps to Join

Applications are ongoing, year-round.

  • 1

    Fill out the interest form

    Basic information about you. No more than 2 minutes to finish.

  • 2

    Join the weekly newsletter

    You're automatically added upon finishing the interest form,
    so you're always up to date on all things Student Horizons.

  • 3

    Meet with a co-founder

    What you're looking for, what the role actually asks of you, and whether there's a fit.

  • 4

    Work through onboarding

    Understanding, in-depth, who we are and how we operate,
    and how your role will embed within our organization.

  • 5

    Join the program

    Congratulations! You're a Student Ambassador for Student Horizons.
    You'll be connected to your students to begin supporting them.
